Privacy Policy — BMI Calculator

Package com.tahatec.bmi_calculator · Published by Tahatec · Effective 17 October 2026

BMI Calculator (“the app”) calculates body mass index for adults and BMI-for-age
percentiles for children and teens aged 2–19, together with body fat, ideal weight,
calorie, macro, water and waist-ratio tools, a goal planner and a weight history. This policy
explains what the app does with the information you enter.

Your measurements stay on your device

Everything you enter — height, weight, age or date of birth, sex, name, waist, neck and
hip measurements, goal weight and your weight history — is stored only on your device and is
never uploaded by the app. The developer has no access to it. You can delete
history entries in the app, and exporting your history as a CSV file happens only when you
choose to share it, to a destination you pick.

If Android backup is turned on for your Google account, Android may include the
app’s stored data in that backup. You can turn backup off in Android settings.

What leaves your device

Advertising. The app shows banner and full-screen ads from Google AdMob, with Meta
Audience Network as a mediation partner. To select and measure ads, these providers may
collect your device’s advertising ID, your IP address and the approximate location it
implies, and device, app and ad-interaction information. In the European Economic Area, the
United Kingdom and other regions where it is required, the app asks for your choice through
Google’s User Messaging Platform before any ad is requested, and you can change it at any
time from Ad privacy options in the app, which appears where it is required.

Google’s policy: policies.google.com/privacy (https://policies.google.com/privacy)
How Google uses data from apps that use its services: policies.google.com/technologies/partner-sites (https://policies.google.com/technologies/partner-sites)
Meta’s policy: facebook.com/privacy/policy (https://www.facebook.com/privacy/policy)

Analytics. The app uses Google Analytics for Firebase to see which features
are used. Events record which calculators and screens are used, the unit system, and the
BMI category of a result (for example “healthy” or
“overweight”; for the child and teen calculator, the child weight category and
whether age came from a birth date or a number of years). Events
never contain a height, weight, age, date of birth, name or BMI value. Firebase also records some information automatically: an app-instance
identifier, the device model, the operating-system and app versions, the app language, and an
approximate country derived from your IP address, along with standard events such as the first
open and the start of a session. You can turn analytics off at any time with Share anonymous usage data under
More → Privacy; this does not turn off crash reporting.

Crash reporting. Firebase Crashlytics receives a report when the app
crashes: the error and stack trace, the device model, the operating-system and app versions,
and an installation identifier.

Reminders and messaging. Daily weigh-in reminders are optional, scheduled
on your device, and request notification permission only when you switch them on. The app
also includes Firebase Cloud Messaging, which may register a messaging token for the
installation, but the app does not currently subscribe you to any announcements.

Health information

Results are estimates for general information and are not a medical diagnosis. The only
health-related value that leaves your device is the BMI category in analytics, described
above, which is not linked to your name or measurements.

Permissions

- Internet and network state — ads, analytics and crash reports.

- Notifications — weigh-in reminders, requested only when you turn them on.

- Run at startup — re-scheduling your reminder after the phone restarts.

- The advertising and Firebase components add the advertising ID, Privacy Sandbox
(ad services), install-referrer and wake-lock permissions.

What the app does not do

- No account, sign-in or registration.

- No upload of your measurements, profile or history.

- No access to your contacts, photos, files, microphone or location.

- No sale of personal data.

Children

The app is not directed at children under 13 and does not knowingly collect personal
information from them. Measurements entered in the child and teen calculator stay on the device;
only the resulting weight category is sent to analytics, as described above, and it can be
switched off.

Your choices

- Turn analytics off: More → Privacy → Share anonymous usage data.

- Change your ad choice: More → Privacy → Ad privacy options, where offered.

- Reset or delete your advertising ID: Android Settings → Privacy → Ads.

- Remove everything the app stored on your device: uninstall it, or clear its storage from
Android Settings → Apps.

- For data held by Google or Meta under their own identifiers, use their controls linked
above, or write to us and we will help where we can.
